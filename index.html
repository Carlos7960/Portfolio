Questions & answers

Things worth
knowing.

What does the subscription include?

Your website access and the agreed services described in your individual agreement. Hosting, maintenance and support are only included when explicitly stated.

Do I need my own domain?

A custom domain is recommended. Domain registration and hosting arrangements are discussed before the project starts.

Can I request changes?

Initial customisation is agreed before work starts. Further changes can be quoted separately.

What happens if I cancel?

Access and any handover arrangements follow the written cancellation terms agreed before purchase. You retain rights to your own supplied content and materials.

Who is Nova for?

Nova focuses on small businesses and entrepreneurs looking for a professional online presence.
