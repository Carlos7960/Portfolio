Questions & answers

Things worth
knowing.

What does the monthly subscription include?

The subscription provides access to your Nova website while your subscription is active. Exact hosting, maintenance and support arrangements are specified in your agreement.

Do I pay for my own domain and hosting?

Yes. Domain registration and hosting are separate purchases that you manage and pay for directly.

Can I request changes?

Initial customisation is included in the setup process. Additional changes can be quoted separately.

What happens if I cancel?

Access to the Nova website ends under the agreed cancellation terms. You retain rights to your own business content and supplied materials. Nova retains ownership of its underlying designs and platform.

Who is Nova for?

Nova is designed for small businesses and entrepreneurs who want a professional online presence without a large initial investment.
